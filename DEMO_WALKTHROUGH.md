# HUMANIE v0.9 — Demo recording guide

## Before recording
1. Start the app and sign in.
2. Use synthetic demo records only.
3. Confirm health endpoint and version.
4. Prepare Food, Jobs, Education, and Evaluation Lab.

## 66-second shot list
- 0–8s: District console and all three domains; state data is synthetic.
- 8–18s: Food aid C-101, candidates, approval and reason.
- 18–30s: Jobs J-201, employer matching and support.
- 30–40s: Education E-302, scholarship and support.
- 40–48s: Dispatch, receipt mismatch, complaint and audit log.
- 48–60s: Evaluation Lab; discuss synthetic precomputed floor sweep.
- 60–66s: Explain that records are not proof of impact; real pilot requires partner agreement, privacy review and field validation.

## Checklist
Show Jobs and Education, keep screen readable, capture continuous footage if possible, and do not imply real deployment, signed partners or proven impact.
