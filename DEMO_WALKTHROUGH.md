# HUMANIE v0.9 — 66-second Datanova demo recording guide

## Recording status
This is a recording script and shot list. The actual screen recording must be captured by the presenter from the running application; no recording is represented as completed in this package.

## Before recording
1. Open the browser demo (`humanie_demo.html`) or start the app locally and sign in with a demo account.
2. Use only synthetic demo records; do not show personal or real beneficiary information.
3. Confirm the API health endpoint reports version 0.9.0 (if using the app).
4. Have the Food aid, Jobs and Education tabs and the Evaluation Lab ready before starting capture.

## 66-second shot list and narration
The recording must visibly show all three domains (food, employment, education), not only food.

- 0–8s — District console, then click through the **Food aid / Jobs / Education** tabs. “HUMANIE is a district coordination prototype for food, jobs and education. All records are synthetic.”
- 8–18s — **Food aid:** open case C-101, show candidates and scores, type a reason, click Approve. “The system recommends; a named human approves and records why.”
- 18–30s — **Jobs:** open case J-201, show candidate employers with skills fit, approve with a reason, click Attach support (transit pass). “For employment, matching also weighs skills and barriers such as transport.”
- 30–40s — **Education:** open case E-302 (scholarship), show candidate providers, approve with a reason, attach support (data stipend / transit pass). “The same workflow places people into training and scholarships.”
- 40–48s — **Delivery and complaints:** dispatch 40, enter 35 received so the discrepancy flag appears, add a complaint, then show the Audit log. “Every action is recorded; mismatches are flagged.”
- 48–60s — **Evaluation Lab (food only):** show the proportional baseline beside the 30% floor, then move the slider to 40%. “In this synthetic urban stress test, the 30% floor lifts minimum coverage from 0% to 30%; weighted coverage moves from 52.90% to 50.45% against a 43.69% baseline. At 40%, urban is 46.53%. These are precomputed results; no optimiser results exist yet for jobs or education.”
- 60–66s — Close. “A delivery record is not proof of impact. A real pilot needs partner agreement, legal review, a privacy impact assessment and supervised field testing.”

## Recording checklist
- Capture one continuous 66-second screen recording where possible.
- Confirm the footage shows the Jobs and Education tabs and one approved case in each, as the deck (slide 10) promises.
- Keep the browser zoom readable; hide unrelated tabs and notifications.
- Do not imply real-world deployment, signed partners, live integrations, or proven impact.
- If the live demo fails, play the pre-recorded clip and continue with the same spoken caveats.
