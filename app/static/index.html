World Bank / FAO country-year data

Fetches official World Bank Indicators API series. Target indicator is FAO-sourced prevalence of undernourishment (national estimate).

Formal food-resource allocation model

Linear program: maximise weighted served need subject to stock, transport-budget and demand-cap constraints. Community inputs are synthetic demo values.

Exploratory national undernourishment model

Random Forest regression using lagged country-year population, GDP per capita (PPP) and unemployment. Chronological holdout. Not causal and not subnational.







Opportunity intervention simulator
Change assumptions to explore hypothetical training and placement scenarios. These effect sizes are user-supplied assumptions, not measured causal impacts.
