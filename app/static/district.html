From a recorded need
to an accountable outcome.

District pilot workspace for pseudonymous case intake, provider capacity, explainable matching, human approval, delivery confirmation, complaints and operational impact reporting.

Prototype safeguards: Use fictional or properly authorised test records only. Do not enter names, phone numbers, addresses, identity numbers, health details or other direct identifiers. Matching is a recommendation, not an eligibility decision. Every approval requires a human reason. This is not yet approved for live public-benefit delivery.

Workspace access

Create a local pilot workspace or sign in to an existing one. Each account sees only its own workspace records.

District operational pulse

How to operate this pilot
  1. Register a pseudonymous need record.
  2. Register a provider and its available capacity.
  3. Generate candidate matches.
  4. Review the rationale and approve or reject with a reason.
  5. Record dispatch, then confirm received quantity.
  6. Log complaints and document their resolution.
  7. Review the audit trail and operational indicators.
